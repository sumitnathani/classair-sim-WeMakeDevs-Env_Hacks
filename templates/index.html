ClassAir

Classroom purifier verification, powered by AWS
📖 How to use this (first time? read this)
PM2.5 at start

The particulate matter reading (µg/m³) right when the purifier turns on. Get this from a PM2.5 sensor or air quality monitor in the room.

PM2.5 at 10 minutes

The same reading, taken 10 minutes after the purifier started running. Shows how fast it's cleaning the air.

PM2.5 at end (30 min)

The reading after 30 minutes of continuous purifier operation — where the air quality levels off.

Decline rate (first 10 min)

How many µg/m³ the PM2.5 dropped per minute, on average, during the first 10 minutes. Calculate as (start − 10min reading) ÷ 10.

Room volume (m³)

Length × Width × Height of the classroom in meters. A typical Delhi classroom is roughly 150-200 m³.

Purifier rated CADR (m³/hr)

"Clean Air Delivery Rate" — printed on the purifier's box or spec sheet. Tells you how much air it can clean per hour when working at full capacity.
